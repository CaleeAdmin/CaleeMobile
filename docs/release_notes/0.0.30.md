# 0.0.30

## What's New

- Subscribe to and manage local device calendars, with a clearer limit when you
  reach the maximum number of subscribed calendars.
- Attach photos and documents to calendar events, then open or share them from
  the event.
- Calee now asks for fewer permissions on Android — the app no longer requests
  broad access to your photo and media library.
- Various stability and layout fixes across the home, calendar and chores views.

## Internal notes

- Removes the broad storage/media permissions that Google Play rejected in
  0.0.29. The final APK/AAB are inspected by
  `scripts/check_android_release_permissions.sh` in CI and in the signed-build
  workflow, so the regression cannot silently return.
- Android signed builds are obfuscated with split debug info; native and Dart
  symbol bundles are uploaded with the release artifacts.
